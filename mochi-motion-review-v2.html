Mochi action review · V2

One cooking action at a time.

These are real, generated multi-frame loops—not a single image being shaken. The action order follows the Omurice recipe from preparation through serving.

No Miso layer is used here, so there is no double exposure or overlapping paws, pans, or food.

1Prep & cut

Lift the knife, slice the scallion, separate the rings, then move forward.

Cut ingredients first.

2Fry chicken-rice

Scoop, turn, mix in ketchup, then cook until the rice is evenly pink.

Build the rice base.

3Make soft egg

Pour, swirl the pan, gently draw in the setting edge, and keep the top glossy.

Do not fold yet.

4Fold & plate

Set rice to one side, fold the egg, tip the pan, and release it seam-side down.

Move it onto the plate.

5Sauce & serve

Start the ketchup line, draw a clean zigzag, then finish the plate.

Final garnish.
